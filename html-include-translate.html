About Rock Cannons

English: Rock cannon

Welsh: Carreg cannan / cerrig cannan

A traditional celebration device in North Wales, made by drilling holes in big rocks and filling with black powder.

235 sites were recorded in Gwynedd by 2001.

Used for weddings, royal visits and festivals in the 18th-19th centuries.
